PROBLEM/QUESTIONS: 
 
1. Question 1: SUB-CLO012-3, Weight (100%) 
 
This exam is related to the final deep learning project. For the midterm exam, you are required to present 
and report the dataset you have collected for your final project. The dataset must be primary data, 
meaning it is collected directly by your group (e.g ., through surveys, experiments, interviews, or 
measurements). Then, in a PDF file, answer the following questions. 
 
1. Dataset Description 
a. Explain the background of your chosen case study. 
b. Describe how you collected the dataset (method, num ber of samples, variables 
collected). 
c. Justify why this dataset is relevant to the chosen case study and suitable of applying 
neural networks. 
2. Data Preparation 
a. Show the preprocessing steps you applied  
b. Provide a clean version of your dataset. 
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) 
a. Present descriptive statistics for the main variables. 
b. Provide at least 5-10 visualizations and interpret each visualization with depth: not just 
describing, but explaining possible patterns, anomalies, correlations, or biases. 
c. Explain insights you discovered from the EDA.  Highlight insights that may influence 
feature selection or modeling strategy. 
4. Reflection
